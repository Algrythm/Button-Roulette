## DESCRIPTION:

You've been kidnapped along with your friend.


You find yourself sitting opposite each other at a table in an abandoned warehouse. A controller with 10 buttons sits in front of each of you. The man explains, "One of these buttons will kill you when you press it. The other 9 are safe. You need to pull out the death button, but if you pull the wrong button out, you die. You can press as many buttons as you'd like, narrowing down your options, but raising your chance of pressing in the death button. Your luck determines your fate. 


Every time you press 3 buttons in, you will be given a card. The card gives you an ability that can benefit you, or benefit your opponent. Play them wisely.

Good luck!"


Get out alive; only one of you will.





# Instructions

LMB: Push down button


RMB: Pull out button


Press area on the bottom right of the table to access cards. Cards can only be played when it is your turn.
